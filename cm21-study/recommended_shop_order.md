# Recommended shop order and alternatives

**Research recommendation: use the lower-proximity-dependence package as the default for review; prefer the conditional package only with practice-room evidence for its positional skills. Do not purchase until current-client conditions, available hints, and shop totals are checked. No points were spent.**

These are the most useful measured alternatives, not a proven exhaustive optimum. Costs below use the supplied final totals. Gold totals include the white prerequisite; ◎ totals include ○. Where the shop shows an incremental upgrade, buy the white/○ first and then only the difference shown here. The total is verified arithmetically, but hint discounts were not independently observed. Native unique Lv4 stays equipped for zero additional SP.

## Purchase priorities supported by this experiment

The six-item opening core (Winter ○, Firm ○, Front Savvy ○, Groundwork, Early Lead, Victoria) costs **522 SP**. Three guaranteed event/style greens satisfy Groundwork at the start; Groundwork itself still passes a WIT check (about 91% here). Outer Post is gate-dependent and should not substitute for a guaranteed third green. Buy greens before Groundwork in the review list so its setup is explicit.

Turbo Sprint repeatedly beats white Sprinting Gear in isolated comparisons, but judge its **incremental** 160 SP against other purchases. Professor, gold proximity skills and ◎ greens have measured upgrade gains in `upgrade_ablation.csv`; those gains are context-dependent. Standard Distance and Moxie improve HP but did not improve solo finish times here. Focus is small in this isolated timing experiment; early position effects are not adequately valued by this solo test. Prideful King is incompatible with an always-leading assumption; no zero-value claim for every lobby is justified.

Exact player discount validation still needs the current shop/hint levels. Data metadata supplies undiscounted bases and skill groups, not the trainee’s current discounted total. Never add a listed gold total on top of its white total.
